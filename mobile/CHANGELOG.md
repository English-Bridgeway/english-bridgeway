# English-Bridgeway app: what's new

Each `## <version>` section becomes the notes of that GitHub Release (see
`.github/workflows/mobile-release.yml`). Write for learners, newest first.

## 1.1.0

- **Welcome pages**: three pages on first install show what the app does: lessons, calling Bridget, and your dashboard
- **Your dashboard**: after you log in, Home shows the course you're working on with Resume, your streak, lessons done, quiz average and this week's activity
- **Shortcuts**: one tap to call Bridget, Grammar, Vocabulary, Speaking, Quizzes, E-books, Saved words and Courses
- **Update popup**: the app tells you when a new version is out and downloads it for you

## 1.0.0 (2026-10-25)

The first release of the English-Bridgeway app for Android. It uses the same account,
courses and progress as englishbridgeway.com.

### Talk with Bridget, your AI English tutor
- **Call screen**: talk to Bridget face to face, like a video call. She shows when she's speaking or listening, and the conversation appears as live captions.
- **One natural voice**: everything she says plays in her own warm voice (ElevenLabs), English and Marathi alike.
- **Mistakes made clear**: when you make a mistake, your wrong words are struck out in red and the right words are shown in green, with the full corrected sentence.
- **Explained in Marathi**: she explains in Marathi when and why we use the right word in real life. Then she says the correct English sentence for you to repeat, and asks you the next question.
- **Mic auto-on**: the microphone opens again by itself when she finishes, so you can just keep talking. Turn it off with the ear button.
- **Real-life situations**: free talk, job interview, restaurant, shopping, travel, doctor and office.

### Learn
- Grammar lessons with rules, examples, common mistakes and practice.
- Vocabulary with search, categories, saved words and word of the day.
- Spoken English dialogues with Listen buttons.
- Quizzes with instant feedback, a review and your best scores.
- E-books: read the course PDFs in the app or download them (with a paid course).
- Blog articles.

### Courses and your account
- Course pages with the full curriculum and free trial lessons.
- Log in or sign up, add a profile photo, see your plan, usage and orders.
- Works offline with the lessons it last downloaded. Pull down to refresh.

### Good to know
- Needs Android 7.0 or newer and an internet connection for Bridget and your account.
- Speak Live (calls with other learners) is on the website for now.
